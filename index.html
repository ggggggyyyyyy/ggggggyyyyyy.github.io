Research

Making model behavior more dependable as the world changes.

I work across statistics and machine learning to build language-model systems that reason reliably, act safely, update knowledge over time, and adapt efficiently. My current work centers on three questions.

01

Reliable reasoning and agent safety

How can conformal methods and uncertainty-aware decision making improve LLM reasoning and control the risks of AI agents?

02

Knowledge updating

How can models absorb long streams of edits without forgetting earlier knowledge or damaging unrelated behavior?

03

Efficient adaptation

How can structured adapters and quantization improve model efficiency while retaining capability?

Pause and Reflect: Conformal Aggregation for Chain-of-Thought Reasoning

Yu Gu*, Zijun Yu*, Vahid Partovi Nia, Masoud Asgharian

A fully inference-time framework that combines quality-weighted aggregation with conformal risk control, providing finite-sample control of confident errors in multi-path LLM reasoning.

90.1% selective accuracy on GSM8K with fewer than 5% abstentions.

Beyond Hard Writes and Rigid Preservation: Soft Recursive Least-Squares for Lifelong LLM Editing

Xinyu Wang*, Sicheng Lyu*, Yu Gu*, Jerry Huang, Peng Lu, Yufei Cui, Xiao-Wen Chang

RLSEdit casts lifelong model editing as an online quadratic problem with soft preservation constraints and an efficient recursive least-squares update.

EvoEdit: Evolving Null-space Alignment for Robust and Efficient Knowledge Editing

Sicheng Lyu, Yu Gu, Xinyu Wang, Jerry Huang, Sitao Luan, Yufei Cui, Xiao-Wen Chang, Peng Lu

A sequential null-space alignment method that preserves both original and previously edited knowledge while limiting catastrophic interference.
